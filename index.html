About Me

Hey, I'm Swypyy. I’m just starting out on a journey to learn all the creative skills I’ve ever been curious about—drawing, animation, coding, and building games, apps, websites, and software. I’m not a master at any of this yet; I’m literally learning every skill I like from scratch as I go.

This site is where I'll be sharing that learning process—my web novel, early art, small game and app experiments, and whatever project I pick up next. No master plan, just diving into cool ideas, figuring them out, and seeing where it goes.

If you want to follow along while I learn and build things, welcome in :)
