CHAPTER 3: The House of Echoes

The phone kept blaring.
Leo didn’t wait this time. Frustrated and terrified by the repetition, he grabbed the receiver and shouted into it, "Hello?! Who is the—"
Before he could finish, the frantic voice on the other end cut right over him, breathless and urgent:
"Just hear me out. Do not open the front door. If someone knocks, if someone horns... never open it. Please—"
Thud.
The line went dead.
Leo stared at the phone. Without thinking twice, he turned around, sprinted straight to his bedroom, and dove under the bedsheets. He pulled the thick blanket tight over his head, curling into a ball. He was shaking so hard he could barely breathe. I'm not looking. I'm not answering. I'm just going to stay here.
Suddenly, the overhead lights flickered and died. Total darkness.
Thump. Thump. Thump.
Three heavy knocks hammered against the front door, then abruptly stopped.
Leo squeezed his eyes shut, pressing his hands over his ears, and started praying in a whisper. God, please... please make it stop...
A few seconds later, a sharp crashing sound echoed from deep inside the house—like heavy objects falling over near the storeroom or bathroom. Panic clawed at his chest, but Leo forced himself to stay under the blanket, squeezing his eyes shut, trying desperately to force himself to sleep through the nightmare.
And just like that... everything went quiet.
After what felt like hours of agonizing silence, the lights suddenly flickered back on.
From downstairs, the audio of the TV show he had been watching earlier blared to life—the characters speaking loudly—before cutting off dead after just a few seconds.
Then, the house phone started ringing again.
Leo froze under the blanket. He held his breath.
The ringing didn't last long. Suddenly, the sound cut off mid-ring, as if someone downstairs had picked up the receiver and started talking.
Someone is down there.
Driven by a mix of fear and sheer curiosity, Leo slowly crawled out from under the blanket. He crept out of his bedroom and peered over the railing of the second-floor stairs, looking down into the dark ground floor.
As he took a step down, the hallway lights flickered violently and went out. Pitch black.
Walking slowly and quietly in the dark, Leo reached the bottom of the stairs. His breath hitched.
In the dim light filtering through the front window, he saw someone standing near the living room.
Leo squinted. His blood ran ice cold.
It was another version of himself. A copy.
The copy was standing completely still by the window, wearing the exact same clothes, slowly and fearfully pulling back the curtain to look outside.
Suddenly, the copy’s eyes went wide with absolute horror at whatever was out on the street.
Panic seized Leo. He turned around to run, but in the pitch black, his foot slammed hard into a loose object on the floor, sending it crashing down.
Clatter!
Leo froze, his eyes widening. Wait... that falling sound... that was me.
The loud noise startled the copy downstairs, echoing through the empty house. Realizing that he was the one who had caused the mysterious crashing sound earlier in the loop, Leo's mind spun out of control. Nothing made sense anymore.
Panicking completely, Leo didn't look back. He bolted through the dark, scrambled back up the stairs, threw himself into his bedroom, and dove back under the blanket, shivering in the dark.
