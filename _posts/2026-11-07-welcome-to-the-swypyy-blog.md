Welcome to the **Swypyy Journal**—a place to share what I’m building, what I’m learning, and the ideas that inspire me to keep experimenting.

I’m Swypyy, an independent creator who enjoys exploring technology and turning curiosity into practical projects. I’m still learning as I go, and I see each project as an opportunity to try something new, solve a problem, and improve my skills.

## What I’m building

My projects range from small experiments to interactive web apps. A couple of examples are:

- **Swypyy Messenger** — a browser-based chat experience built around temporary rooms and real-time conversations.
- **Anonymous Drop** — a community space for sharing anonymous posts and comments.

I also use this site as a home for my projects and a way to make the things I create easier to explore. These projects are works in progress: I’ll keep refining them, learning from what works, and sharing updates along the way.

## What you’ll find here

I plan to use this journal for:

- **Project updates** — new features, improvements, and releases.
- **Build notes** — decisions, challenges, and lessons from making things.
- **Experiments and ideas** — concepts I’m exploring, including projects that are still taking shape.

I’m interested in a wide range of topics, so this journal may cover different kinds of work. The thread connecting them is curiosity: learning how things work and seeing what I can make with that knowledge.

## Thanks for reading

This is the beginning of the journal. If you’re curious about what I’m working on, take a look around the site and check back for future updates. I appreciate you being here while I learn, build, and share the process.
